# app-icon-auto-reload — Workspace changes require one restart

1. The member signs in. Belonging to a single organization, they skip the picker and go straight to reviewing the resources their workspace provides.

2. JuggleWork detects that the organization has a custom desktop identity and prepares its name, wordmark, icon, and accent color.

3. Most of that identity applies immediately. Only when something is left for startup to finish does JuggleWork download an available application update before asking the member to continue.

4. JuggleWork names exactly what one restart will finish applying. The member can restart now or continue straight to the workspace.

5. JuggleWork restarts once, installs any staged update, and opens directly into the selected workspace with its branding applied.
