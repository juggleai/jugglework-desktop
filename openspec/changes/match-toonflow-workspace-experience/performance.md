## Measurement Contract

Representative fixtures are generated by `toon-workspace-performance.ts`: small has 24 nodes, 12 documents, and 50 assistant events; large has 1,000 nodes, 100 documents with about 20 MB of searchable text, a 2,000,000-character document probe, and 2,000 assistant events.

Recorded release thresholds are: first-open domain preparation under 120 ms; retained hidden serializable state under 8 MiB; median canvas adapter frame under 16.7 ms; large-document line reveal under 80 ms; bounded direct search under 250 ms; assistant event decode under 100 ms and 2 MiB; aggregate lazy Toon/XYFlow/CodeMirror production chunks under 650 KiB gzip.

`tests/toon-workspace-performance.test.ts` records and enforces runtime measurements for both fixtures. `scripts/toon-bundle-budget.mjs` measures production output after `pnpm build`. The measured large fixture is below the P2 thresholds, so node, asset, file-tree, and search-result virtualization and optional persistent search indexing are intentionally not added. Existing bounded result pages and server scan byte/file limits remain the scale safety boundary. Virtualization or indexing must be introduced only after this harness reports a threshold regression, and must then preserve selection, keyboard navigation, minimap bounds, search reveal, exclusions, cancellation, and retained state.
